Overview
[
edit
]
Balkhash Airport is classified as a national aerodrome in the latest AIP.
[
1
]
It is capable of accepting
Ilyushin Il-76
,
Tupolev Tu-134
,
Tupolev Tu-154
,
Antonov An-8
,
Antonov An-12
,
Antonov An-24
,
Ilyushin Il-14
,
Ilyushin Il-18
,
Yakovlev Yak-40
,
Yakovlev Yak-42
, and
Antonov An-2
aircraft. It can also accept light aircraft and helicopters of all types.
The length of the runway is
2,502
m ×
40
m (8,209
ft ×
131
ft)
. A dirt runway was also used in the past, but it is now closed.
Military aircraft are also based there. In the last few years, civil aircraft rarely use this airport and the airport is mainly used by the sky diving club of the city of Balkhash and the military.
