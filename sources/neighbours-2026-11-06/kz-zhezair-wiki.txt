Zhezkazgan Air
, branded as
ZhezAir
, is an airline which operates scheduled and charter flights in
Kazakhstan
. It is headquartered at
Jezqazğan Airport
in
Jezqazğan
and operates domestic flights out of its base.
Until August 2012, the Government of Kazakhstan had the majority of stocks in the company. On August
29, 2012 the government stocks were offered for purchase at the
Kazakhstan Stock Exchange
.
