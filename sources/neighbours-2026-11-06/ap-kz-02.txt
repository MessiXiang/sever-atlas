1

It has a small terminal with five airliner parking spots. It has serviced the
Ilyushin Il-86
. In 2023, the airport served more than 900,000 passengers.

]
On

, the airport was officially renamed in order to commemorate

Hero of the Soviet Union
and

soldiers during
