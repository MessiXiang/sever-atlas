Helicopter Tour to Khan-Tengri Peak

Description
Embark on an exciting adventure in the heart of the Tien Shan! A helicopter flight over Kyrgyzstan’s largest glacier and the famous Merzbacher Lake offers unforgettable views.
Khan Tengri Peak will reveal its full majestic beauty.
Experience the atmosphere of an alpine Base Camp at over 2500 meters, with comfortable tents, tasty meals, and a glimpse into the daily life of mountain expeditions. This trip is perfect for those seeking memorable experiences and inspiration among the towering peaks.
Program
Day 1. Bishkek city – Karkyra camp
Your adventure begins early in the morning in Bishkek. We will travel along the northern shore of Issyk-Kul Lake.
The journey takes about 8 hours (470 km), with a stop for lunch at a cozy café along the way.
By evening, you will arrive at Karkyra camp at the foot of the mountains, where you will stay in comfortable double tents and enjoy a delicious buffet dinner.
Day 2. Helicopter flight to South Engilchek Base Camp
After breakfast, an impressive helicopter flight over the heart of the Tien-Shan awaits. Beneath the helicopter, you will see green valleys, alpine meadows, and snow-capped ridges. You will fly over the Tien-Shan’s largest glacier, Engilchek, as well as the mysterious Merzbacher Lake, which appears and disappears depending on the season.
The helicopter will land at the South Engilchek Base Camp, offering incredible views of Victory Peak (7439 m) and the legendary Khan-Tengri Peak (7010 m).
Dinner and overnight stay at the Base Camp.
Day 3. South Engilchek Base Camp – Karkyra camp – Bishkek city
In the morning, enjoy breakfast followed by a helicopter flight back to Karkyra yurt camp.
Afterward, a transfer to Bishkek awaits. The tour ends here, but the memories of majestic peaks, glaciers, and the fresh mountain air will stay with you for a long time.

What to know before the tour
Altitude:
Base camps are located above 2500 m above sea level. Mild acclimatization is possible – being in good physical condition is recommended.
Weather:
Mountain weather changes quickly. Days can be warm, but nights are cool.
Accommodation:
Overnight stays are in tents.
Meals:
Full board is included (breakfast, lunch, dinner).
Communication:
Mobile coverage and internet are limited in the mountains.
Physical activity:
Daily walks along the route take up to 4 hours. No special mountaineering skills are required.

Tipping in Kyrgyzstan: rules and recommendations
In Kyrgyzstan, tips are not mandatory, but are always appreciated by service staff. Tips are not usually included in the price of services.
In cafes and restaurants, you may see an additional charge of 10-15% on your bill, but this is not a tip – it is part of the staff’s salary.
It would also be nice to tip the guide, driver, and porters. This is a great way to express your gratitude for their work and attention.
As a guideline, the average tip is approximately USD 7-10 per person per day, which is distributed among all staff members – drivers, guides, porters.
However, the amount of the tip is always at your discretion and depends on the quality of service and your means.
