About Us

Mongolian Airways took flight in 2017 with a mission dedicated to cargo transportation. Initially, we operated two Boeing 737-300SFs, steadily carving our niche in the aviation industry. Today, we expanded our fleet to include Airbus A330-200 and A320-200 aircrafts, enabling us to offer international passenger operations.
