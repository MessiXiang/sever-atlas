Business Aviation Operators - Mongolia

A business aviation operator is a company holding an air operator certificate (AOC) and entitled to fly commercially on the aircraft in its fleet. The directory for Монголия lists 7 such operators with bases at 1 locations. The operator is responsible for the crew, maintenance and safety standards, so the choice of operator decides the rules your flight is run by.

According to publicly available data, there are seven business aviation and helicopter operators operating here, with their main bases in Ulaanbaatar. Below is a directory with contact information, specialization, and fleet for each operator.

Questions about operators: Mongolia

How many business aviation operators are there in Mongolia?

According to open data, there are seven operators based in two cities: Ulaanbaatar (Chinggis Khaan International) and Ulaanbaatar (Buyant-Ukhaa International). The list is verified against open sources and updated as changes occur.

What types of aircraft do operators have in Mongolia?

Local companies' fleets include the Airbus Helicopters H125, Airbus Helicopters H145T2, Eurocopter EC145, Cessna 208B, Cessna 208B EX, and Robinson R66. We select the specific aircraft for the task from this fleet and from aircraft in neighboring countries.

Why look at the operator's AOC?

An air operator certificate (AOC) is issued by the aviation authorities of the country of registration and defines flight regulations, crew requirements, and dispute resolution procedures. Flights are always operated by the operator, not the broker, so their approvals are verified before a booking is confirmed.

Market data and sources

  • IS-BAO (IBAC): an air operator certificate confirms the right to fly commercially, while the independent standard shows how procedures are built inside the company
  • ARGUS: an operator audit checks crew experience, safety history and fleet condition — before booking, not after
  • NBAA: three quarters of business aviation operators fly a single aircraft, so availability is checked for every individual flight
