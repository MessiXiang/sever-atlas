Introduction
Since 2023, Alpha Aurora Aviation has been guided by one simple principle, which is aviation should be safe, reliable, and professionally managed.
We were founded to meet the growing need for reliable helicopter services in Mongolia. From the beginning, our focus has been on safety, operational excellence, strong engineering, and professional service.
Our Journey
Key Milestones
2023
Alpha Aurora Aviation established in Mongolia.
MCAA Part 135 Air Operator Certificate obtained.
MCAA Part 145 Aircraft Maintenance Organization established.
Operations commence with an Airbus H125, Airbus EC145 C2, Airbus H145 D3 and Robinson R66.
2024
Three new Airbus H125 helicopters delivered by Airbus Helicopters.
External load operations introduced in support of Mongolia’s mining and exploration industries.
2025
Expansion into third-party aircraft maintenance, engineering support and operational services.
