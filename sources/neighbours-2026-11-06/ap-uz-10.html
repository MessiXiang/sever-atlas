Zarafshan Airport (IATA: AFS, ICAO: UZSN) is an airport of entry in Zarafshon, Navoiy Region, Uzbekistan.

Facilities

[edit]

The airport has one concrete runway which is 1,820 metres (5,971 ft) in length.[1]
