About us

About the Company

Asman Airlines LLC is a new state-owned airline of the Kyrgyz Republic and a wholly owned subsidiary of JSC “Airports of Kyrgyzstan”.

The airline offers affordable, convenient, and comfortable air transportation across the Kyrgyz Republic, combining competitive pricing with a high level of service onboard modern Dash Q400 aircraft, designed to accommodate 80 passengers.

The route network covers key cities across the country, including Bishkek, Osh, Manas, Tamchy, Kerben, Karakol, Kazarman, Razzakov, Talas, Naryn, and Batken, as well as international destinations such as Almaty, Tashkent,Khujand.

Regardless of the purpose of travel—whether for business or visiting family—the airline ensures a reliable and comfortable journey, allowing passengers to discover the beauty and diversity of Kyrgyzstan.

Asman Airlines — your reliable path to new horizons.

Mission

Asman Airlines aims to make air travel within the Kyrgyz Republic accessible and convenient for all, ensuring a high level of safety and comfort at every stage of the journey.

The company’s primary mission is to connect cities and regions, bring people closer together, and create new travel opportunities, contributing to the development of tourism and the economy of the Kyrgyz Republic.

Values and Objectives

Accessibility

The airline strives to provide affordable air travel for all categories of passengers by offering competitive fares.

Comfort and Reliability

Ensuring a high level of safety and comfort on every flight through the use of modern aircraft and a professional team.

Innovation

Implementing modern technologies and solutions to improve service quality and simplify the travel experience.

Route Network Development

The airline plans to further expand its route network, including the launch of international flights.

Starting from 2027, the airline plans to operate international routes, subject to obtaining the necessary approvals and in compliance with international aviation standards.

Strengthening Market Position

The airline aims to become a leading carrier in the domestic aviation market of the Kyrgyz Republic by offering a high level of service and optimal conditions for passengers.

In the future, the airline also plans to enter international markets and expand its network of destinations.

Support for Tourism and Economy

The airline contributes to the development of tourism and the economy of the Kyrgyz Republic by expanding opportunities for travel and business both domestically and internationally.

Continuous Service Improvement

The main objective is the continuous improvement of service quality to ensure that every flight leaves passengers with a positive experience.

The airline strives not only to meet but to exceed customer expectations by offering modern and safe air travel solutions.
