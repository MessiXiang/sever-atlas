Business Aviation Operators - Mongolia

Questions about operators: Mongolia
How many business aviation operators are there in Mongolia?
According to open data, there are seven operators based in two cities: Ulaanbaatar (Chinggis Khaan International) and Ulaanbaatar (Buyant-Ukhaa International). The list is verified against open sources and updated as changes occur.
What types of aircraft do operators have in Mongolia?
Local companies' fleets include the Airbus Helicopters H125, Airbus Helicopters H145T2, Eurocopter EC145, Cessna 208B, Cessna 208B EX, and Robinson R66. We select the specific aircraft for the task from this fleet and from aircraft in neighboring countries.
Why look at the operator's AOC?
An air operator certificate (AOC) is issued by the aviation authorities of the country of registration and defines flight regulations, crew requirements, and dispute resolution procedures. Flights are always operated by the operator, not the broker, so their approvals are verified before a booking is confirmed.
