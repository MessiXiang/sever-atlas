Asman Airlines is recruiting crews for subsequent retraining on the BC Dash 8 Q400 type.
Aircraft commanders (PIC)
Minimum qualification requirements:
1
A valid CAA KR air transport pilot certificate or recognition of a foreign air transport pilot certificate with aircraft type approval;
2
For foreign pilots, two letters of recommendation must be provided, one of which must be from a previous employer;
3
Local/foreign pilots must not have a record of dismissal for disciplinary violations;
4
A total flight time of at least 3,000 hours;
5
Minimum flight time as a PIC – 1,000 hours, or at least 1 year as a PIC, depending on the situation;
6
No more than 1 year after the last flight;
7
Maximum permissible age – 60 years, minimum permissible age – 30 years;
8
Not liable for military service;
9
Proficiency in English (in accordance with the CAA KR requirement – ICAO level 4);
10
Negative test result for psychoactive substances, in accordance with ICAO requirements;
11
Good character, no criminal record (certificate of no criminal record), successful completion of the Asman Airlines simulator test;
12
Successful completion of familiarization and induction into company procedures, prior to commencing flights as a captain;
13
Be competent to perform duties, in accordance with the Asman Airlines Flight Operations Manual;
Co-pilot
Minimum qualifications:
1
Valid Commercial Pilot License;
2
For foreign pilots, two letters of recommendation must be provided, one of which must be from a previous employer;
3
Local/foreign pilots must not have a record of dismissal due to disciplinary violations;
4
Not liable for military service;
5
Possession of a diploma of specialized (or higher) aviation education;
6
Good command of English (passing a language proficiency assessment before being assigned to perform duties, the minimum language level in accordance with the requirement of the Civil Aviation Authority of the Kyrgyz Republic is ICAO Level 4);
7
Minimum - 500 hours of total flight time, depending on the type of aircraft on which the co-pilot has previous experience (at the discretion of the management of Asman Airlines LLC);
8
No criminal record, good reputation.
