Our fleet has welcomed a new addition!
7 April 2025
Today marked the ceremonial arrival of the fourth LET L-410 aircraft, which will be operated on the following routes: Nukus – Muynak – Nukus, Tashkent – Shakhrisabz – Tashkent, and Tashkent – Zaamin – Tashkent. You can view the full flight schedule and purchase tickets at silk-avia.com, via the mobile app, at all international airports across the country, or at the Uzbekistan Airways Sales office.
