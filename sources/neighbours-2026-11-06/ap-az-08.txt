Lachin International Airport
(
Azerbaijani
:
Laçın Beynəlxalq Hava Limanı
) is an airport in
Lachin
,
Azerbaijan
.
Development
[
edit
]
The construction of the airport began in May 2021 and is expected to be completed in 2024.
[
1
]
[
2
]
It will be the highest altitude airport in Azerbaijan at 1,700-1,800 meters above sea level. The Lachin International Airport will be Azerbaijan's third air harbor in the areas gained in the
2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war
, in addition to the international airports in the
Fuzuli
and
Zangilan
districts. The airport is located at the
Qorçu
village of the Lachin district, slightly more than 30 and 60 kilometers from Lachin and
Kalbajar
cities, respectively, and about 70 kilometers from
Shusha
city.

Azerbaijan
Ilham Aliev
,
Turkey
Recep Tayyip Erdoğan
and

Shehbaz Sharif
.
