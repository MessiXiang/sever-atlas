air taxi

Exclusively from Vanilla Sky – first air taxi service in Georgia! We offer express flights to different destinations in Georgia by selecting appropriate aircrafts. Our vessels comply with all international standards of flight safety.

 

Vanilla Sky operates its own fleet of small comfortable aircrafts, providing maximum flexibility, comfort, safety, and confidentiality of flights. Our aircrafts can be used for a wide range of tasks, including transportation of passengers and freights, short air trips and air shooting, and as a special purpose transport. Vanilla Sky fleet and airdrome are located in Natakhtari, a 20 minute drive from Tbilisi.
