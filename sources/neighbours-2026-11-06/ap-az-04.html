Balakan Airport (Azerbaijani: Balakən Hava Limanı) is an airport serving Balakan,[1] the capital of the Balakan District of Azerbaijan.

Facilities

[edit]

The airport resides at an elevation of 935 feet (285 m) above mean sea level. It has one runway designated 13/31 with an asphalt surface measuring 1,209 by 37 metres (3,967 ft × 121 ft).[1]
