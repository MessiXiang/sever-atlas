FLIGHT SCHEDULE

Natakhtari - Mestia - Natakhtari One way: Adult 90 GEL; children (3-12) 63 GEL; infants (0-3) free

Natakhtari - Ambrolauri - Natakhtari One way: Adult 50 GEL; children (3-12) 35 GEL; infants (0-3) free

Natakhtari - Batumi - Natakhtari One way: Adult – 125 GEL; children (3-12) – 88 GEL; infants (0-3) - free

Mestia - Kutaisi - Mestia One way: Adult 50 GEL; children (3-12) 35 GEL; infants (0-3) free

Flights to Mestia destination operate from Natakhtari and Kutaisi airports.

Flights to Batumi and Ambrolauri  destinations operate from Natakhtari airport..

Natakhtari is 23 km from Tbilisi but we provide free transportation from Tbilisi center to Natakhtari airport.

Pick-up point for the flights from Natakhtari airport is under the "BUDGET" sign, on the opposite side of the bicycle statue that is near Radisson Blu Iveria. It’s blue Mercedes Sprinter. Transfer leaves exactly 2 hours prior to the flight, so make sure you are there before that time - the driver will not wait for anybody. Baggage allowance is 15 kg per passenger (including hand luggage).

if after choosing destination/date/number of tickets on our website you click on the looking glass and do not receive any information (such as flight time, price) it means there are no more or not enough tickets left.

If you are purchasing round trip tickets and after clicking on the looking glass you do not see 2 flights, it means that on one of the routs on your preferred date there are no more or not enough tickets left.

When you choose your destination/date/number of tickets and click on the looking glass and the flight appears,unless there are two flights to choose from, DO NOT CLICK ON THE FLIGHT, just click CONTINUE. Then fill out all fields and continue with payment.Please keep in mind that the transaction is not successful unless you receive your tickets on the email you provided in the appropriate field.

Please be advised that changes of passenger, date and destination in purchased tickets will not be allowed.
