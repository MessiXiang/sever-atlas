Kulob International Airport
(

:

international airport
serving

, the third largest city of the country, and the capital of

of

. Situated approximately
6

Russia
.
History
[
edit
]
Construction of the airfield began in 1977 during the Soviet period. Prior to the
dissolution of the Soviet Union
and Tajikistan's independence, the facility operated as a local domestic airport built to handle short-range passenger aircraft such as the
Yakovlev Yak-40
.
[
2
]
Following the civil war, the facility underwent extensive reconstruction: the single runway was widened from 35 metres (115
ft) to 45 metres (148
ft) and extended from 2,000 metres (6,600
ft) to 3,000 metres (9,800
ft). On 30 December 1995, the airport landed its first medium-haul passenger jet, a
Tupolev Tu-154
operated under the command of Mirzo Mastongulov.
[
2
]
In 1998, the airport was granted full international status, inaugurating an official government and VIP delegation hall that was subsequently renovated in 2015. Regular scheduled international operations commenced in 2002, predominantly serving labour migration and business transit between Khatlon Province and the
Russian Federation
.
[
2
]
Between 2006 and 2014, the airport underwent phased upgrades:
A dedicated departures terminal was opened in 2006
[
citation needed
]
Capital resurfacing and runway reinforcement works were finished in 2008
[
citation needed
]
A second floor was constructed atop the main passenger terminal in 2014, increasing passenger handling capacity by 200 travellers per hour
[
citation needed
]
Subsequent apron expansions and modern instrument navigation beacons were installed to accommodate commercial narrow-body jet airliners.
[
2
]

Ground transportation
[
edit
]
The airport is positioned approximately
6
km (3.7
mi)
north of central Kulob, accessible via the A385 highway. Local transit is handled by private motor vehicles, official airport taxis, and fixed-route shared minibuses (marshrutkas). The facility is also situated near the southern terminal station of
Tajikistan Railways
, providing onward rail links across Khatlon Province towards
Bokhtar
and
Dushanbe
.
