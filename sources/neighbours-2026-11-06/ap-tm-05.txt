Mary International Airport
is the third largest international airport in
Turkmenistan
. In March 2009, the airport was expanded with a new terminal and opened a US$5 million, two-story, 3,500-square-metre terminal and upgraded its status from provincial to international airport.
[
3
]
The airport is located 6
km north-east of the centre of
Mary
.
