Tajikistan, officially the Republic of Tajikistan, is a mountainous
landlocked
country in
Central Asia
.
Afghanistan
borders it to the south,
Uzbekistan
to the west,
Kyrgyzstan
to the north, and
People's Republic of China
to the east.  Tajikistan also lies adjacent to
Pakistan
, separated by the narrow
Wakhan Corridor
. Its
capital city
is
Dushanbe
.
