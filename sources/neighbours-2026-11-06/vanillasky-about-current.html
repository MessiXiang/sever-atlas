About us

Vanilla Sky Georgia offers special, diverse and high-quality services.

Our company, established in 2008, is a leader in the tourist sector of Georgia. Thanks to our ten years’ experience, we can offer you a wide range of unique, customized, comfortable and high-quality services, including:

  • Our private airdrome in Natakhtari
  • Domestic flights
  • Air taxi
  • Tourist services
  • Vanilla Sky complex
  • Hotel accommodation and transportation throughout Georgia
     

Regular Domestic Flights in Georgia:

Take our regular domestic flights to Batumi, Mestia and Ambrolauri. You can buy the tickets online exclusively on our website or in our office.
 

Air Taxi / Air Trips:

Fly to any destination in Georgia by Vanilla Sky’s aircrafts meeting all international standards. You can rent an aircraft for any period and any purpose (passenger transportation, air trips, air shooting).

Spend unforgettable moments with Vanilla Sky!
 

Travel Services:

Plan your holidays with Vanilla Sky! Our guests can choose among a wide variety of tour packages to any destination in Georgia, take exclusive air trips with a unique opportunity to interact with people and culture of the place they choose to visit. We are happy to offer transfers, hotel accommodation and other services.  Enjoy high-quality customized services with Vanilla Sky team!

 

Vanilla Sky Complex:

We wanted to create a place for you to relax and feel at home, and so we did! We have built a modern, safe and comfortable leisure complex Vanilla Sky in Natakhtari, just a 20-minute drive from Tbilisi, where you can enjoy:

  • A restaurant
  • A hotel
  • An outdoor pool
  • An airdrome
  • Domestic flights
  • Air trips
  • Tourist services

Whatever you plan - a voyage, an event, a business meeting, a stay in a hotel, a dinner or an air trip - Vanilla Sky will help to realize your plans with comfort and safety.
