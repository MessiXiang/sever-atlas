Zaisan Airport (Kazakh: Зайсан Әуежайы / Zaisan Äuejaiy, IATA: SZI, ICAO: UASZ) is classified as a national aerodrome in the latest AIP.[1] The length of runway is 1,505 m × 35 m (4,938 ft × 115 ft).
