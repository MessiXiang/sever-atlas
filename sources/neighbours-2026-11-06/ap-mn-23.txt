Tavan Tolgoi Airport
(
Mongolian
:
Таван толгой нисэх буудал

Tavan tolgoi nisekh buudal
) is an
airport
serving the
Tavan Tolgoi mine
in
Tsogttsetsii, Ömnögovi
,
Mongolia

Information
[
edit
]
Tavan Tolgoi Airport is located 100 kilometers northeast of the regional capital of the
Dalanzadgad
