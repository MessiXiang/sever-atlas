History
[
edit
]
Tajik Air
resumed flights on the
Dushanbe
–
Khorog
– Dushanbe route from September 30, 2024. The flights operate three times a week on an
AN-28
aircraft. The flight time from Dushanbe to Khorog is 1 hour and 10 minutes.
