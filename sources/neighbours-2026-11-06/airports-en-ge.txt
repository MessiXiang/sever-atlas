Georgia is a

Eurasia

Black Sea

Russia
, to the south by
Turkey
and
Armenia
, and to the east by
Azerbaijan
. Georgia covers a territory of
69,700 square kilometres (26,911
sq

and its population is 3.7 million.
The country is divided into nine

autonomous republics
, and the
capital city
of
Tbilisi
.
