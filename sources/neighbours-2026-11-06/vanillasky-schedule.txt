If you are purchasing round trip tickets and after clicking on the looking glass you do not see 2 flights, it means that on one of the routs on your preferred date there are no more or not enough tickets left.
When you choose your destination/date/number of tickets and click on the looking glass and the flight appears,unless there are two flights to choose from, DO NOT CLICK ON THE FLIGHT, just click CONTINUE. Then fill out all fields and continue with payment.Please keep in mind that the transaction is not successful unless you receive your tickets on the email you provided in the appropriate field.
