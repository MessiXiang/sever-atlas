Vanilla Sky
(also known as Service Air or AK-Air Georgia) is a privately owned
airline
headquartered in
Tbilisi
,
Georgia
and based at
Natakhtari
. It was founded in 2008. The airline is part of "Service Air" Company, a subsidiary of Vanilla Sky International Tour Operator. Flights are subsidized by the state to increase mobility and tourism in Georgia with US$5.3 million as the reported amount that the airline was offered by the Georgian Airport Union.
