Balkanabat Airport (Turkmen: Balkanabat aeroporty), also known as Nebit Dag Airport, was a provincial airport located 4.5 kilometres (2.8 mi) southeast of Balkanabat in Turkmenistan. In May 2025, a new Balkanabat International Airport was opened and replaced the old airport.

History

[edit]

The opening ceremony of the new Balkanabat Airport terminal was held on 7 November 2004. The terminal building is spread over 3,500 square metres and has a capacity of handling 200 passengers per hour. The runway can handle aircraft weighing up to 150 tonnes.[4]

In July 2021, 20 kilometers from the city of Balkanabat, construction began on the new Balkanabat International Airport,[5] which opened in May 2025.[6]
