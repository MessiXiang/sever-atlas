Khujand International Airport
(
IATA
:
LBD
,
ICAO

UTDL
)
is an airport serving
Khujand
, the second-largest city in
Tajikistan
. Khujand was formerly known as
Leninabad
(during the Soviet era); hence the
IATA code
LBD
. It is located out of the city, in the nearby town of
Buston
.
Facilities
[
edit
]
The airport resides at an elevation of 442
m above
mean sea level
. It has one
runway
designated 08/26 with an
asphalt
surface measuring 3,200 x 50
m.
The new passenger terminal was opened on
1 October 2019
.
[
2
]
In 2024 the airport served 779,201 passengers, which is 22.1% less than in 2023 when 1,025,537 passengers were served.
