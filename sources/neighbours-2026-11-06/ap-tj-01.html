Ayni Airport (Russian: Аэропорт «Айни»; Tajik: Фурудгоҳи Айнӣ) is an airport serving Ayni in the Sughd Province in Tajikistan. It does not seem to be coded in IATA or ICAO lists,[2] but had the code АЙН [1] in the former USSR internal system.
