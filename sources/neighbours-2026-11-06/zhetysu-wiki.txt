Activity
[
edit
]
Between January and July 2009, the airline served 8,671 passengers and performed 181 flights. The coefficient of utilization of passenger seats was 68%.
[
1
]
In 2006 airline served 15,261 passengers.
[
2

The airline operates only one daily route from
Taldykorgan
to
Usharal
. The airline also operated a route from Taldykorgan to
Almaty
, but due to unprofitability, the management decided to close the route. As of December 11, 2013 the Taldykorgan-Almaty route was reopened due to reconstruction of a highway.
