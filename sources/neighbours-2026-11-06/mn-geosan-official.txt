Why GEOSAN
The domestic operator the majors rely on.
Our first contracts in 1998 were near Oyu Tolgoi and Gatsuurt for BHP Billiton. Three decades on, clients still choose us for the same reason: we get the aircraft to the block and the data holds up.
Full survey capability
Nearly the entire range of ground and airborne surveys available today: magnetic, radiometric, gravity, IP/resistivity, EM and borehole logging, all in-house.
International-standard kit
Industry-standard acquisition systems and a team recognised for technical expertise in geophysics, geodesy, geology and aviation.
Built for remote terrain
Two Cessna Grand Caravans and the ability to open field aerodromes mean we reach sites across the Gobi, mountains and steppe — and abroad.
